SpeccyScreen.isScanLines read the scan-line knob back through a private read(key) that returned Object and cast it
to Number, a getter left from before the screen's settings became knobs: nothing called it, in the project or in any
plugin, so the cast stood in dead code. The knob already says its own value to whoever shows it, which is why no
caller needed the getter. Removing the four dead getters and the read they shared removes the cast with them.

model: procedure-instead-of-model
