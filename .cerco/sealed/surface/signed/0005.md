## Need

A 128K snapshot of a 48K game can hold, in banks nobody pages in, the game loaded before it: a snapshot of Silk Worm was recognised as The Addams Family at 0.55 because banks 4, 6 and 7 were still Addams. Recognising a game needs the RAM a processor has in front of it (banks 5, 2 and the one port 7FFD pages in), and only the format knows where the file keeps that port.

## Considered

`SnapshotFormat#ram(byte[])`: gives every bank joined together, without saying which ones are paged in, so what an earlier program left behind cannot be told from the game.
