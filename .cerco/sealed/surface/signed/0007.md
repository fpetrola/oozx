## Need

A plugin that plays a game's beeper music on an AY (the viewer's AyStage, brought as an oozx plugin) has to hear every OUT to the speaker with the instruction that made it and where the stack stood: one routine plays every sound of a game, and only where it was called from tells the music from the effects. Nothing sealed tells anyone of a port write, let alone with the processor's PC and SP; Processors owns both the processor and its IO, so it is the one that can say it without the instructions or the CPU changing.

## Considered

Processors.ReadListener#read(int, int): void tells of port reads only, and with the port and the value alone; a write is not a read, and neither carries the PC or the SP the sound needs. Reading the PC and the stack from the CPU in the plugin instead would be asking the processor from outside, which is what this seam exists to avoid.
