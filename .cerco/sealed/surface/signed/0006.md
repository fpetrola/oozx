## Grows

  - `com.fpetrola.z80.cpu.Z80Cpu#runFetches(int, Runnable): long`

## Need

Playing an RZX recording needs the machine to run until the processor has made a given number of fetches (the recording measures frames in M1 cycles, counted by R). The sealed surface only offers one instruction at a time (Z80Cpu#execute), so the player counted R itself around every step, from outside the processor, and the JIT shaped that outer loop differently in each JVM: Exolon took 22.6 to 24.2 s. Run as one stretch inside the processor it takes 19.8 to 21.1 s (Fuse: 22.2 s), with identical frames and instructions.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.z80.cpu.Z80Cpu#execute(): void` runs one instruction and says nothing of how many fetches it made: the caller has to read R before and after, and recognise LD R,A by its bytes, which puts the processor's own fetch count outside it and is the slow loop measured above.
