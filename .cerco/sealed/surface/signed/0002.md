## Need

A device window with a DAC (Covox, SpecDrum, the Currah µSpeech) shows a meter when folded, and expanded it
should show a vertical form where each field has its label: the status and the volume that every such box has,
and then what each box adds, like the µSpeech's ROMs and a line to make it speak. The frame that builds the form
is in the kit and the boxes are plugins, so the plugins need a way to add a labelled row to that form.

## Considered

- `com.fpetrola.oozx.speccy.devices.Equipment#open(): MachineFrame` hands the desk a whole window, built by the
  plugin; a box could build its form entirely by itself that way, but then every DAC box would repeat the
  status, the volume and the meter that the kit's DacFrame already owns, and each would lay them out its own way.
