## Need

The Kempston Mouse plugin has to keep the program's pointer under the hand, so it has to know which pixel of the Spectrum is under a point of the machine's picture. Only the picture knows where and how big it draws the screen (border on or off, whole-pixel scale, centred), so the question goes to it; today the mouse measured in desk pixels times 0.70 and drifted.

## Considered

`com.fpetrola.oozx.speccy.windows.MachineWindow#picture()` gives the picture as a JComponent, which says its size but not where the screen sits in it nor at what scale; adding the question to MachineWindow would make EmulatorInternalFrame answer it, in ZXSpectrumDesktopApp.java, which is at its file ceiling. `SpeccyScreen#getZoom()` is the zoom asked for, not the scale painted.
