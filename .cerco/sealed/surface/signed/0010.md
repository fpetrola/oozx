## Grows

- machine/core
  - `com.fpetrola.oozx.Extension#devices(): java.util.List<Class<? extends com.fpetrola.oozx.speccy.peripherals.Peripheral>>`

## Need

A device brought with *bring* while a machine is running never reaches that machine: its registry was filled once, from what the Extensions bound into Set<Peripheral> when the injector was made, and a binding cannot be added to a made injector (a child that binds Set<Peripheral> again is refused by Guice, BindingAlreadySet). So the window clips on and says "not plugged into a machine". If an Extension says which devices it brings, the registry makes them itself with the machine's injector - all of them when the machine starts, and those of a jar that arrived later the next time it is asked - so the Set<Peripheral> binding and the hand-over in Speccy.init go away and there is one place that says it.

## Considered

- `com.fpetrola.oozx.config.RomsOfItsOwn#files(): Map<String,List<String>>`: it is the same shape - a jar saying as data what it brings, so it is known without installing anything - but it says ROM files by machine name, not devices; stretching it to devices would give one role two meanings.
- `com.fpetrola.oozx.Extension` is sealed with no members of its own: what it brings is only said through Module#configure, which binds into an injector that does not exist yet, so a machine already made has nobody to bind into and nothing to ask.
