## Need

A sound plugin attached to an emulator window has to hear every write to the speaker port together with the
instruction that made it and the top of the stack, which tells one routine of a game from another. Processors
already tells reads through loose public methods; registering for writes needs the same, and both belong to one
promise about the traffic through the ports, on every core, without touching instructions or the CPU.

## Considered

- `com.fpetrola.oozx.speccy.modules.z80.Processors.ReadListener#read(int, int)`: only reads, with no instruction or stack.
- `com.fpetrola.oozx.speccy.modules.z80.Processors.WriteListener#written(long, int, int, int, int)`: signed already, but nothing sealed lets anyone register one.
