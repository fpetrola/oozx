## Need

The card that shows what is known of a game (GameCard, in the games plugin) has to show what other plugins have of that game: its pokes today, its music and its map later. Nothing sealed lets a plugin say "I have N of these for this game, and this is the page where they are used", so today the card names the pokes plugin's class directly, which is the dependency this project avoids everywhere else.

## Considered

- `com.fpetrola.oozx.speccy.devices.WhatGameThisIs#show(Desk.Game): void` shows a game, but it is one answer from whoever knows the catalogue: it gives other plugins no way to add their part.
- `com.fpetrola.oozx.speccy.devices.MachineTool#use(EmulatorWindow): void` is a plugin's button on a machine's toolbar: it is about a window, not about a game, and has no count and no page to place inside another window.
