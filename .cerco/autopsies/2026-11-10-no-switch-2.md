## Cause

A knob says what sort of control shows it with Knob.Kind (CHOICE, SWITCH, NUMBER), and that sort decides two things:
which control the settings window builds for it, and how its value is read back from the text a settings file kept.
Kind was a bare list of names, so both decisions lived outside it, each as a switch over the three constants:
KnobRows.controlFor built a combo box, a check box or a slider, and ScreenSettings.apply parsed a double, a boolean
or kept the string. Every new sort of knob would have had to be remembered in both places. The constants now carry
it: each reads its own written value, and each picks its own control from a Knob.Controls the window implements once
(a visitor over the three sorts), so the window keeps Swing and Knob stays free of it.

model: variation-as-data

## Principle

Polymorphism over type tests: what is decided per constant of an enum is the constant's behaviour, and when the
decision belongs to another layer (Swing) the enum offers a visitor instead of being switched over.

## Candidate rule

rule: enum-decided-across
The constants of an enum of the project decided (switch, ==) in two or more members anywhere in the project. It is
the candidate already in cerco 0.14.0, which found this case across KnobRows and ScreenSettings and still finds the
Dock case at machine/ui/src/main/java/com/fpetrola/oozx/speccy/windows/AttachedFrame.java:360.
