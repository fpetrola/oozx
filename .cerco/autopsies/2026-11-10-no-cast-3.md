window of it, and turn that window into a player. openFor did the last step with a cast and opensFor with a pattern,
because each copy had been written on its own. With the walk asked of WhatIsPluggedIn.opening and the empty window
found in one place (opensFor), openFor narrows what it was handed instead of casting it. The narrowing itself is
still there: Equipment.open() hands out a MachineFrame, and a player only says it is one by implementing Opens.
